# P30 submission consistency audit

## Slide headlines

- **Nominal ACO vs exact search (P9).** The three-year search evaluated 168 canonical rotations in Kansas, 211,394 in Telangana and 16,914 in Makueni. Across 30 trials per farm and preset, one colony run hit the exact score 21–30 times (327/360 overall). The highest score from five independent runs hit 360/360, with zero gap at the reported precision. [Nominal verification](optimizer.md)
- **Historical checks (P15, P19 and P20).** Of 70 comparisons, 58 produced Spearman correlations and 18 of the 58 descriptive bootstrap intervals excluded zero. P15 contributed 12 correlations and 4 intervals excluding zero, P19 contributed 36 and 11, and P20 contributed 10 and 3. The comparisons reuse observations, and the intervals are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. They examine NASA input consistency and regional yield associations. Rotation quality still needs field evidence. [All comparisons](backtest.md#all-correlations-across-p15-p19-and-p20)
- **Largest and contrary associations.** In P19, Makueni short-rains integrated NDVI had Spearman ρ +0.83 with rainfall anomaly (95% bootstrap interval +0.26 to +1.00, n=8) and −0.81 with rainfall deficit (−0.99 to −0.22, n=8). These reflect the same rainfall signal. Kansas warm-season heat days and integrated NDVI had ρ −0.60 (−0.77 to −0.24, n=24). Telangana monsoon rainfall anomaly and integrated NDVI had the opposite sign, ρ −1.00 (−1.00 to −1.00, n=3). In P15, Makueni short-rains heat days and NDVI amplitude had the opposite sign, ρ +0.95 (+0.75 to +1.00, n=5). In P20, Finney winter-wheat water deficit had ρ −0.61 (−0.87 to −0.21, n=20) for reported non-irrigated yields and −0.62 (−0.81 to −0.32, n=27) for the overlapping all-practice series. [Seasonal results](backtest.md#seasonal-comparison-results) · [Yield back-test](backtest.md#p20-yield-back-test)
- **Learned yield check (P25/P27).** On pooled Kansas data, the USDA district holdout used 3,215 county-years across nine districts. RMSE in bu/acre was 11.202 for county mean, 10.758 for linear trend, 9.562 for ridge and 9.034 for random forest. The check describes historical weather–yield associations in Kansas county averages. Crop rotation quality is outside this evaluation. [District, year and county results](ml_model.md#results-by-subset-and-split)
- **Sensitivity to assumption changes (P22).** The 512 joint draws per farm yielded exact top scores spanning 0.522–0.854 across combinations. Across farm–preset combinations, the share of draws with a changed winner ranged from 74.4% to 96.5%; material changes ranged from 59.0% to 92.8%. Kansas varied 121 inputs, Telangana 150 and Makueni 104. These ranges depend on the tested assumptions. [P22 inputs and results](sensitivity.md#results-by-farm-and-preset)
- **Preliminary risk-aware picks (P28b).** Across 128, 256 and 512 draws, rotations stayed the same in 8 of 12 farm–preset combinations for expected score, 6 of 12 for conditional CVaR and 2 of 12 for minimax regret. At 512 draws, 18 of 36 picks were within the existing objective tolerance of their runner-up. Makueni's income-preset conditional-CVaR pick passed feasibility rules in 108 of 512 draws (21.1%). These selections depend on the tested assumption ranges. [P28b exact results](robust_optimization.md#exact-results)
