# Additional bean nitrogen benchmarks (P49)

Illustrative comparisons. Validate locally before using any value.

The catalog uses **22 kg N/ha** for common bean. Chui's Machakos field-yield fits span **0.7 to 56 kg N/ha**. The catalog value falls within the reported Machakos span. The earlier Katumani trial reported **44 to 80 kg N/ha**. Their reported spans overlap at 44 to 56 kg N/ha. These bounds describe published conditions and carry no field-level uncertainty interval. [Catalog](../data/crops.csv) · [University of Minnesota Extension, Soil biology, Table 1](https://extension.umn.edu/natural-resources/conservation/agricultural-soil-and-water/soil-biology) · [Chui thesis](https://erepository.uonbi.ac.ke/server/api/core/bitstreams/e03bc575-0a4d-40d0-8d40-3bb49f9fd7e7/content) · [P45 Katumani comparison](literature_check_p45.md#nadar)

The catalog converts the 20 lb N/acre edible-bean first-year corn credit to a rounded value. The transfer to Makueni is an assumption, validate locally.

Fertilizer equivalence matches a maize response to an N fertilizer curve. Nitrogen in residues, soil mineral N and maize uptake quantify other parts of the system. Yield equivalence can reflect water, other nutrients and crop-break effects.

## Earlier Katumani reference

Following maize fertilizer equivalents were 44 kg N/ha for 1982/83 short rains, 80 kg N/ha for 1982 long rains. Sandy clay, Oxic Paleustalf (Chromic Luvisol). Bean residue return or removal for these particular comparisons remains unverified. Yield-matched fertilizer equivalent from a fitted continuous sole-maize response curve. [Nadar and Faught (1984), East African Agricultural and Forestry Journal, Figure 2, Tables II–VI and pp. 130, 135–136](https://pdf.usaid.gov/pdf_docs/PNAAX822.pdf)

## How to read the Machakos discrepancy

Page 156 gives the full site-by-season list. Page 147 assigns different residue groups to the Kimutwa values. The Table 37b equation labels support a separate check for one short-rains season. Following Appendix Table 21, we matched the zero-added-N maize-after-bean fitted intercept to the lower nonnegative root of the continuous-maize curve. This is a recalculation of the same experiment. The thesis's contradictory assignments and dependence on a fitted yield response limit what can be inferred about residue management. [Chui thesis](https://erepository.uonbi.ac.ke/server/api/core/bitstreams/e03bc575-0a4d-40d0-8d40-3bb49f9fd7e7/content)

Fertilizer equivalence depends on the crop, soil, weather, residue management and maize response. It can include rotational effects unrelated to nitrogen. Field-level credit and rotation suitability at the Makueni coordinate remain unverified.
