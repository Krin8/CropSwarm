# CropSwarm demo

## Script · about 2½ minutes

| Time | Screen | Talk track |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | US High Plains B | “A farmer often has several plausible rotations and limited time to compare them. CropSwarm starts with the rotation they already use and brings NASA observations and local assumptions into one comparison.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Rainfall and vegetation | “NASA POWER shows the seasonal water pattern. Terra MODIS provides vegetation context. This point's 2019 CDL class is fallow even though MODIS shows a spring peak. The products cover different footprints, so we keep the discrepancy visible.” |
| 0:45–1:05 | Inputs and priorities | “The farmer can edit crops, soil water and the share of irrigation demand they can meet. Here I put more weight on water. The chart re-ranks the displayed rotations.” |
| 1:05–1:30 | Compare rotations | “CropSwarm compares the existing rotation with four distinct strategies. Each shows how it trades off water, nitrogen, erosion, climate fit, income and risk. Open one to see its evidence.” |
| 1:30–1:55 | Warmer and drier | “The percentiles show a range of modeled outcomes. Wins show how frequently each strategy scored highest among the displayed rotations under the selected preset.” |
| 1:55–2:15 | Telangana, then Makueni | “Telangana's repeated paddy keeps its rotation-rule warning. Makueni's cropland status is unverified. These flags matter before making a change.” |
| 2:15–2:30 | Optimizer check | “We compared ant colony search with exact enumeration. Every farm and preset reached the exact optimum in at least one seeded run. Validate the results locally. The farmer decides.” |

## Slide outline

1. Describe the decision and the farmer's existing rotation.
2. Show the site's NASA rainfall and vegetation charts alongside the soil context.
3. Adjust the farmer's six priorities.
4. Compare the existing rotation with four alternatives. Show one benefit, one trade-off and one evidence source.
5. Explain the scenario percentiles and wins, then show the Makueni flag.
6. Close with exact optimizer verification and the evidence the farmer needs for a local decision.
