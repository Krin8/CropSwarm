# P48 Makueni cropland and parallel point

The original Makueni coordinate **−1.950, 37.640 remains cropland unverified**. One of four maps with a point label classifies it as cropland. Two of those maps classify the parallel point at **−1.950125, 37.639875** as cropland, meeting the preregistered map agreement rule. The parallel point is **19.7 m** southwest of the original. Both points are illustrative and need local validation. The nominal original coordinate lies on a WorldCover grid corner touching class 30 and class 40. The boundary convention takes the pixel to the east and south at equal coordinates, which returns 30. The parallel point is the nearest eligible native GLAD center, with agreement at an adjacent GLAD center too. The original coordinate's three-decimal precision and the parallel point's short distance matter when interpreting that pixel difference. Coordinates alone leave field boundaries unresolved. [Analysis and boundary tests](../tests/test_makueni_cropland.py)

GLAD maps herbaceous cropland and can include intermittently fallow land within a four-year epoch. WorldCereal targets temporary crops over its annual window and excludes permanent crops and temporary pastures. WorldCover and WorldCereal use overlapping Sentinel observations, while the GLAD cropland extent map uses Landsat. These maps have different definitions, observation periods, pixel sizes and errors. ESA reports **76.7%** global overall accuracy for WorldCover v200. The study has no site-level accuracy estimate. Map agreement leaves current field use, crop identity and management unresolved. [GLAD description](https://glad.umd.edu/dataset/croplands) · [WorldCereal product paper §§2.3, 3.7](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/15/5491/2023/essd-15-5491-2023.html) · [ESA WorldCover validation](https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access)

## Reproduction and project wording

The app reads the new P48 processed output and shows both points in the Makueni Validation panel. The original Makueni warnings in the README, demo and prior outputs still apply at the original point. The new statement applies to the separate parallel context only. Two products map that point as cropland for their observation periods. The existing baseline's maize, common bean and local crop seasons remain assumptions.

Rebuild the analysis offline with `python -m src.makueni_cropland`. The three clipped native-pixel arrays, GeoTIFF URL and ETag records, and the WorldCereal STAC item are checked in under [P48 source evidence](../data/raw/p48/). `python -m scripts.cache_p48_rasters ASSET` can resume an authorized public source download by reusing cached source byte ranges. Bounded retries and ETag checks guard against mixing asset versions. Appropriate asset names are `worldcover`, `glad` and `worldcereal`. The full imagery or byte-range cache is unnecessary for the offline app. ESA WorldCover project 2021 includes modified Copernicus Sentinel data processed by the ESA WorldCover consortium, CC BY 4.0.
