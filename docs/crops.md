# Crop knowledge base

**Illustrative; validate locally.** The crop catalog defines the inputs used to compare illustrative rotations. Local varieties, planting dates, soils and management can change suitability, crop water use and nitrogen carryover. The CSV pairs agronomic parameter groups with source columns. Fields labeled `assumption, validate locally` are scenario inputs.

## Water, weather and duration

`water_need_mm` estimates crop evapotranspiration over one growing period, in millimetres of water. Published reference ranges supply a representative value or the lower endpoint for a short growing period, as noted in each source field. FAO-56 describes the crop coefficient method for estimating evapotranspiration using local weather. The catalog supplies indicative totals. Cowpea uses the midpoint of two published field ET totals from Bangalore, 281.6–288.0 mm, as an illustrative regional reference. The [P23 audit](p23_changes.md) records the source and transfer limits. Paddy's value excludes water for land preparation, flooding, percolation and seepage, which requires separate local estimates. [FAO irrigation training manual, Tables 6 and 14](https://www.fao.org/4/s2022e/s2022e07.htm), [FAO-56 Chapter 6](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0b.htm), [FAO paddy irrigation requirements](https://www.fao.org/4/s2022e/s2022e08.htm).

`min_rain_mm` and `max_rain_mm` describe total rainfall over the assessed growing window for a preliminary climate screen. Effective rainfall and unmet water demand require separate calculations. Likewise `min_temp_c` and `max_temp_c` screen the growing-window mean daily air temperature, while `heat_tolerance_c` is a threshold for counting days whose daily maximum exceeds it. Cowpea's heat trigger, temperature and rainfall bounds, and 85-day growing window are assumptions to validate locally. See the [P24 source recheck](p24_audit.md).

`season_length_days` represents a single illustrative crop cycle from planting to harvest or termination. Winter wheat includes dormancy and can span calendar years. Other crop durations are references or locally unverified assumptions, as indicated in the table. [Kansas State Wheat Production Handbook calendar](https://bookstore.ksre.ksu.edu/pubs/wheat-production-handbook_C529.pdf), [FAO Kenya assessment, Table 4.11](https://www.fao.org/4/t0733e/T0733E05.htm), [ICRISAT repository, Table 10.7](https://oar.icrisat.org/11909/1/Chapter-Legumes-2021-Proof.pdf).

## Scoring inputs

- `n_credit_kg_ha` is an illustrative following-crop nitrogen credit. `n_demand_kg_ha` is an assumed nitrogen requirement for comparison and excludes any fertilizer recommendation. Where available, first-year corn credits from Minnesota Extension were converted from pounds per acre to kilograms per hectare and rounded to whole units. Applying those credits to other crops or climates is an assumption. Credit varies with nodulation, harvest, residues and the following crop. [University of Minnesota Extension, Table 1](https://extension.umn.edu/natural-resources/conservation/agricultural-soil-and-water/soil-biology).
- `cover_score` is a relative erosion protection index. `relative_income_index` is a relative economic scenario input with no currency, prices or yield estimates. Cereal rye assumes termination for cover without sale. [SARE cereal rye guidance](https://www.sare.org/publications/managing-cover-crops-profitably/nonlegume-cover-crops/cereal-rye/).
- `root_depth_class` is a qualitative grouping. For sourced crops it uses the midpoint of the reference depth range and the following illustrative class boundaries: shallow below 0.6 m, medium from 0.6 m to below 1.2 m, and deep at or above 1.2 m. Actual rooting depth depends on soil horizons and restrictions. [FAO-56 Table 22](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0e.htm).
- `disease_family` groups crops by botanical family as a coarse rotation proxy. Actual pest and pathogen hosts can cross families or differ among related crops.

## CSV contract

Load with `src.crops.load_crops(path, farms=farms)` to get typed records indexed by `crop_id` and check baseline membership. If using a generic CSV reader, skip the first comment line. `seasons_allowed` is a JSON array of model labels. Kansas uses `cool` and `warm`, Telangana uses `monsoon` and `post_monsoon`, and Kenya uses `long_rains` and `short_rains`. Labels mark potential windows and feasibility still depends on local dates and conditions. Kansas's single annual rotation slot may represent either a cool- or warm-season crop, whose weather windows differ.

Bare fallow can occupy any slot. Its zero values represent the absence of an active crop, with zero crop demand, nitrogen credit, income and ground cover in this scenario. Climate thresholds and effective root depth do not apply. Bare soil can still lose water through evaporation and face erosion risk.
