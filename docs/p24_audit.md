# P24 final consistency audit

P24 checked the README, submission summary, demo script and app against the committed reference-farm outputs and methodology docs. It also checked the corresponding tables in the optimizer, historical back-test and robustness docs. All results remain illustrative and need local validation.

## Numbers checked against saved outputs

- **Optimizer and alternatives.** All 12 farm–preset rows agree with the saved verification, including hit rates and mean and worst gaps. The canonical search sizes are 168 for Kansas, 211,394 for Telangana and 16,914 for Makueni over three years. Single runs hit the exact optimum 21–30 times per 30 trials. Best of five hit it 30 of 30 in every row. Each reference farm has a baseline and four alternatives that clear the distinctness threshold on at least one of six normalized metrics. [Optimizer](optimizer.md)
- **Robustness.** The grid has 35 cells per farm and preset. All 12 changed-label counts, the 11 nearest changed cells and their adjacent gaps agree with the cached tables. All 11 reported changes qualify as near-ties under the 0.01 weighted-score tolerance, including four Kansas changes with an exact tie on both sides. Telangana water saver keeps its top label throughout. The grid ranges and both the 0.01 tolerance and 0.05 alternative threshold are design choices. [Robustness](stress_test.md)
- **Climate coverage.** The climate cache holds 12,784 daily dates per reference farm from 1991 to 2025. The app's dry-spell lengths, annual rainfall variability, quality-screened composite counts and rainfall agreement values match the assessment records. Checked all 72 farm-year coverage entries for 2001–2024. Each farm has 552 valid MODIS composites, of which 516 passed QA in Kansas, 295 in Telangana and 345 in Makueni. [Data pipeline](data_pipeline.md) · [Annual coverage table](backtest.md#annual-modis-coverage)
- **Historical comparisons.** Checked all 70 comparison cells, including every reported coefficient, interval and paired sample size, plus P15 and P19 directional counts and the available P20 yield years. P15 computed 12 correlations with 4 intervals excluding zero, P19 computed 36 with 11, and P20 computed 10 with 3. Total **58 correlations, 18 intervals excluding zero**. The absent 12 correlations are shown as unavailable. Comparison counts use full-precision endpoints. [Complete tables](backtest.md)
- **Evidence and site flags.** The reference strategies have low overall evidence labels. The Kansas point is modeled with 0% irrigation supply and its spring 2019 NDVI peak over CDL-mapped fallow remains unexplained. Telangana's second season is tentative and the repeated-paddy flag is visible. Makueni remains unverified cropland. [Evidence grades](evidence_grades.md) · [Site notes](decisions.md)

The submission paragraph contains 150 words. Other numbers in the reviewed text include demo timings, UI limits, and runtime instructions. Those describe the demo rather than reported results.

## Verification and limits

The P19 rebuild uses POWER and MODIS with cached season windows and reads no crop catalog. The P20 rebuild reads catalog inputs only for winter wheat, grain sorghum and paddy. Both rebuilds reproduced the committed outputs byte for byte. The numeric crop catalog, scoring, rankings, uncertainty results, robustness outputs and saved evidence grades are unchanged. All displayed reference strategies omit cowpea. The refreshed assumption label applies when a dynamic comparison uses its duration.

All 97 tests passed, including the offline app tests for all three reference farms. Regenerated and verified all 18 PNGs with the browser restricted to local resources. The refresh covers climate, comparison, uncertainty, robustness, validation and evidence views. The capture script now fits the full panels and opens the correlation tally. Checked 151 local README and docs link and image references. [Screenshot index](screenshots/README.md)

Outside the two legacy sources and the already-cited cowpea fallback, external links were not rechecked. The audit cannot resolve the Kansas spring peak, confirm Makueni cropland or establish Telangana's second season. Makueni still lacks a comparable seasonal yield series.
