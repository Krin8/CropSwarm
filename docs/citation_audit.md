# P13 citation audit

Scope covers every external URL originally cited in `docs/crops.md`, `data/crops.csv`, the three `data/processed/context_*.json` files and `docs/decisions.md`, plus replacements. The status applies to the stated use of the source. Crop model values and site interpretations remain illustrative. Direct probes for sources with access problems and the repeat SSURGO query are recorded in [the audit manifest](../data/raw/p13/manifest.json).

The local evidence references were also opened and checked. The [P2 files](../data/raw/p2_evidence/) contain rainfall, site choices and archived subsets. The [P3 manifest](../data/raw/p3/manifest.json) connects ground and terrain context fields to archived SSURGO, SoilGrids, SRTM and USGS payloads. The [P4 coverage report](../data/processed/coverage_p4.json) and [P7 validity outputs](../data/processed/) support distinctions between missing downloads, quality-screened observations and land-cover evidence. Entries explicitly marked `assumption, validate locally` are model choices and make no external verification claim.
