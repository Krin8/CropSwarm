# P8 scoring model

All scores are illustrative and require local validation. Crop demand is the catalog's crop evapotranspiration total. A daily bucket applies NASA POWER rainfall to constant daily crop demand, carries surplus up to the soil available water measured or estimated over 0–30 cm, and discards excess. The bucket begins empty and resets between windows. The configured irrigation share covers a fraction of the remaining demand. The nitrogen balance is previous-crop credit less current-crop demand. The erosion index multiplies SRTM mean slope by the peak daily rainfall within a crop window and the uncovered fraction. This is a simplified comparison index and lacks the calibrated factors needed to estimate soil loss.

Planting starts on the first day of the derived onset month, with a crop-specific duration. Kansas selects the eligible cool or warm window. The natural rainfall window can be extended by up to 45 days for screening. This extension is an assumption, validate locally. Climate fit counts years that meet the crop's temperature and rain ranges and in which at most 10% of days exceed the crop heat threshold. Planting-season mismatches, identical back-to-back crops including the cycle boundary, and a median unmet-water fraction above 80% are hard exclusions. The shortfall threshold deliberately permits stressed rainfed candidates to enter comparison. It is an assumption, validate locally. The display should show their deficit. Baseline scores retain every applicable flag.

Water score is one minus the mean unmet share. Nitrogen scales from minus the largest catalog demand to the largest catalog credit. The erosion score is one minus the ratio to bare cover at the maximum observed daily rain and site slope, with zero slope giving an erosion score of one. Climate fit and the relative income index are already bounded. Risk score deducts equal weight for the uncovered fallow share and the fraction of adjacent active crops in the same disease family. All policy parameters are in `config/scoring.json`. Income is a relative scenario input and lacks local price or yield information. The disease family is a screening proxy.

The local vectorised benchmark took approximately 3.8 million rotations per second on a preloaded batch of 300,000 six-slot Telangana rotations. That rate excludes file loading and historical bucket calculations.
