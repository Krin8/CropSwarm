## Residual diagnosis and fixed methods

- **Diagnostic.** P33's P37-design local scores are absolute errors of a frozen Kansas point model on the first k observed target-district years. They are out of sample for the Kansas model, and the target years fit no center or local correction. They are used directly to choose the local radius. At k = 0 scores come from reserved Kansas districts before the applicable origin. For each target state, learner and k report calibration residual spread divided by test residual spread. Use sample standard deviation of signed errors in bu/acre. Pool district-specific calibration rows across retained districts, counting a shared calibration origin once per district that used it. Use the fixed P37 common test tail. Diagnostic test errors never enter a radius or model.
- **(a) P33 reference.** Keep the existing unweighted split-conformal scores and finite-sample rank.
- **(b) Leave-one-year-out scores.** For each observed calibration year, predict it with the identical frozen source center and score it once. Excluding a calibration year from the center fit is automatic in all P33 protocols. Therefore with the existing frozen point model the leave-one-year-out scores and the reference scores coincide. Do not introduce a locally fitted center solely to make this method differ. Verify the equality and report both.
- **(c) Training-only inflation.** For each P33 source fit, use its reserved Kansas calibration rows, unseen by its point model. Within each of those districts, split its available observed calibration years chronologically into first floor(n years / 2) years and the remaining years. Compute the later-to-earlier sample SD ratio of signed prediction error, requiring at least two rows per part and positive earlier spread. Use the median valid district ratio, floored at 1, as the inflation factor for that frozen model. Use the same factor at every k sharing a source fit. A missing estimate makes this method unavailable. The chronological halves are training-domain pseudo calibration/test samples and do not estimate target shift from target test outcomes.
- **(d) Mondrian by weather regime.** Reuse the P5 regime recipe from `src/diagnostics.py`: total precipitation, mean temperature and mean daily maximum temperature per calendar year, standardization, three k-means clusters, seed 42, ten initializations, wet cluster by rainfall and dry-hot by standardized maximum temperature minus rainfall among the other clusters. Obtain annual inputs only from cached county POWER records. Fit scaler and clusters on complete county-years from the P33 proper-training partition. Assign regimes via that frozen fit to calibration and test county-years. Use the P33 finite-sample radius within each regime. A regime with no scores yields an unbounded interval. Missing annual weather yields an unavailable interval, with sample count reported. If fewer than three distinct complete proper-training weather vectors exist, report this method unavailable.
- **(e) Climate-similarity weights.** Reuse the five P25 seasonal weather features. Fit their scaler only on the P33 proper-training partition. Average standardized features across county rows within each available calibration year and, separately, within each held-out year for the evaluated sample. Give each calibration score in year j weight exp(− squared Euclidean distance between its year centroid and the test year centroid / (2 × 1²)). The Gaussian bandwidth of 1 in standardized units is a fixed design choice. Assign the test point weight 1 at infinity. Take the first ordered score whose cumulative weight reaches nominal level times the combined weight, or use infinity when finite weight is insufficient. There is no bandwidth search or clipping, and this is a similarity weighting heuristic, not a verified likelihood-ratio correction.
