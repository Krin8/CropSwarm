# P48 Makueni cropland verification preregistration

Use the existing Makueni point at `-1.950, 37.640` and the circular 5 km neighborhood centered on it. Five kilometers is a study design choice. Record the native pixel at the point and the cropland share of classified area within the disk. Use pixel-center inclusion and projected or latitude-adjusted areas. Report coverage separately, preserve product years, and keep missing or masked data separate from other land cover. The original farm entry, scorer, parameters and outputs are read-only.

## Products and access

- Check NASA MEaSUREs GFSAD30 Africa cropland extent (nominal 2015, about 30 m), ESA WorldCover 2021 (10 m) and the University of Maryland GLAD global cropland extent epoch 2016–2019 (about 30 m) through verified open publisher or distributor links. Check ESA WorldCereal 2021 temporary crops (10 m) as an additional separately produced classification if its required tile is openly available. Use publisher legends, version identifiers and coverage. Report each attempted product and its access status. A product requiring credentials is skipped. No authentication.
- Include the cached MODIS MCD12Q1 IGBP point label as supporting context. Its existing point request gives no neighborhood fraction. If an already-cached spatial tile is available, compute the fraction using class 12 cropland alone and report mixed class 14 separately. Report class 14 and WorldCover class 40 according to their own legends.
- Only retrieve public cropland or land-cover products and their documentation. Consult the requested NASA POWER product documentation for grid and point-API behavior. Make no new POWER data requests. Reuse partial downloads with range requests where applicable, cache response bytes and provenance, cap retries, and record failures. Source tiles serve reproducibility in the local cache. Save a small clipped classification witness or derived cell-level evidence if needed for offline rebuilding.
- Report native source grids for every variable in the original cached POWER climate record, including UTC rainfall and IMERG where present. Use stated grid alignment where documented. Keep a native-grid inference distinct from any undocumented point-service interpolation or resampling. Reuse the original POWER record only when the candidate is assigned to the same required weather cell under the documented or explicitly qualified grid calculation.

## Verification and alternate site selection

- A point is classified as **cropland by two products** when two independently produced maps label the native point cropland in their published legends. Distinct independently trained products can share satellite observations. Mark that dependence and date/definition differences. Multiple years from one product count as one product. Neighborhood shares describe the surrounding landscape and never certify the exact point. Product agreement is the project's operational verification rule and does not establish current field use, ownership or management.
- Search only within the original 5 km disk. Candidate coordinates come from valid native pixel centers of the coarsest available eligible cropland product. Require a second agreeing cropland classification at the coordinate and at least one agreeing adjacent native center to screen isolated map pixels. Order candidates by distance to the original point, then latitude and longitude for exact ties. Choose the first candidate with defensible POWER cell equivalence. Record evidence and distance; record fewer than two accessible products or absent grid evidence as an incomplete check.
- Build a parallel `makueni_alt` entry in a separate config file if a point qualifies. Use its own coordinate-matched cached MOD13Q1 NDVI (same native NDVI pixel if demonstrated), SRTM and SoilGrids evidence. Do not contact NDVI, elevation or soil data services under the cropland-only retrieval scope. A site that qualifies on maps may be recorded as a pending parallel entry with missing fields explicitly unavailable. Do not substitute original values for a different location or include a pending entry in the main farm picker. If no site qualifies, report it plainly and retain the unverified flag.
- Use the existing P5/P7 seasonality and QA rules and the P5 low-greenness threshold to compare observed NDVI peaks per year, seasonal pattern and low-greenness periods at both points when available. Report missing pixels and the cached observation count. Mixed MODIS pixels, clouds and low greenness cannot establish bare soil. Compare slope and soil context against their cached product footprints.
- Report every accessible or failed product in the write-up, with legend, source, date and limits. Show the result in Validation and the evidence ledger, including gaps and any unresolved grid or context checks. Keep site selection separate from the original scores and previous studies.

All numeric outputs are illustrative and need local validation. Maps describe their observation periods.
