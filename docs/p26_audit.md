Single-run ACO found the exact optimum 21–30 times in each set of 30 trials, or 327/360 across the 12 farm–preset combinations. Each reference comparison shows four distinct alternatives plus its configured baseline, scored on six metrics. [Optimizer](optimizer.md)
- **Back-tests.** There were 70 comparisons, 58 computed correlations and 18 bootstrap intervals excluding zero. By study, P15 had 12 correlations and 4 intervals excluding zero, P19 had 36 and 11, and P20 had 10 and 3. Checked all displayed correlation cells, interval endpoints, paired sample sizes, directional counts and yield record counts. These are checks on NASA input consistency and county or district yield associations. Rotation recommendations still need field validation. [Back-tests](backtest.md)
- **Weather shifts (P16).** Checked the 35-cell grid for each farm and preset, all 12 changed-label counts, and the 11 nearest changed cells and adjacent margins. All 11 nearest changes are near-ties at the 0.01 design tolerance. Kansas contributes four exact ties on both sides of its reported changes. The map applies uniform changes to historical weather. Its results describe the tested cells and make no forecast. [Weather-shift check](stress_test.md)
- **Scenario uncertainty (P10).** Each scenario uses 500 draws and an illustrative ±18% parameter range. Warmer and drier applies +2 °C and a 0.90 rainfall multiplier. The separate irrigation scenarios move the supply fraction by ±0.25, bounded to 0–1. The 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles, win shares and regret are rendered from each saved scenario. Tied scores split the win credit. [Scenario settings](../config/uncertainty.json)
- **Assumption sensitivity (P22).** Checked all 12 rows, top check ranks, weak counts, joint score ranges and the largest rank associations against the three caches. Kansas varied 121 inputs, Telangana 150 and Makueni 104. Each farm used 512 joint draws. Across farm–preset combinations the top label changed in 74.4–96.5% of draws and material changes occurred in 59.0–92.8%. Top scores spanned 0.522–0.854 across the entire study, with changes from the reference top score between −0.192 and +0.155. These ranges depend on the chosen parameter bounds, categories and sampling. [Assumption-sensitivity results](sensitivity.md)
- **Opportunity windows (P17).** Checked all nine farm–cutoff combinations and their fit numerators and denominators. At 0.25, Kansas had a median 96-day low-greenness span in 11/17 assessable years with 92 supporting composites. Telangana had a tentative 32-day span in 2/17 assessable years with four supporting composites. Makueni had no qualifying span. No crop passed the reference screen. At 0.30, the displayed Kansas rye result is 1/15. Its modeled deltas and water use agree with the archive. [Opportunity windows](opportunity_windows.md)
- **Coverage and site facts.** The 1991–2025 POWER record has 12,784 daily dates per farm. The 2001–2024 long MODIS record has 552 composites per site before QA, with 516, 295 and 345 passing respectively in Kansas, Telangana and Makueni. All 72 calendar-year coverage entries and the app's climate assessment summaries match their caches. Kansas uses 0% irrigation in the reference model, and the 2019 spring NDVI peak over CDL-mapped fallow remains unexplained. [Pipeline](data_pipeline.md) · [Site caveats](decisions.md)
